TELL

You are Lulu, climbing an icy mountain. You and the Yeti pick skills in secret, and the fight runs frame by frame until someone is free to act again.

  • Frames: every skill has Startup, Active and Recovery. A fast move lands before a slow one comes out.
  • Your turn comes when you're free. Quick moves (or Wait) give you your next pick sooner, often while the Yeti is still mid-move, and you can see exactly what it's doing and when it hits. It sees you the same way.
  • Ghost: before you lock in, a see-through Lulu plays your pick forward (assuming the Yeti stands still or finishes what it's doing) and tells you damage and who is free first (+ means you).
  • Hitstun & combos: a hit stuns for a while. If you recover first, you can link another hit. Damage and hitstun shrink the longer a combo runs. Counter-hits (hitting someone mid-attack) add hitstun and cost 10 Sanity.
  • Momentum: moves push you with real speed. You keep gliding after a Slide or Step and can attack on the move, jumps arc under gravity, and hits knock you flying. Hard hits bounce off the ice walls at each end of the (much longer) path.
  • Burst: when you're stuck in a combo with a full gauge you get a pick: Burst out invincibly, or hold.
  • Parry: pick the frame a hit will land. Get it within 1 frame and you take nothing, gain Sanity and Light, and act at once. The Yeti's version is Catch.
  • Feint is a skill: when the Yeti gets to act while your skill is still playing, you get a turn too. Press Continue, or press Feint and pick a new skill to cancel into. It costs no Light but needs 5 turns to recharge. The Yeti can feint the same way, and it learns how often you do.
  • Sanity sets each fighter's emotion, and every emotion has a passive that limits moves. Click a fighter to read it.
  • Light pays for specials: +1 every 24 frames, up to 3. Ice Wall and Ice Armor halve damage and stop counter-hits. Ice attacks Chill: +4 frames on the next move.
